5 Common Mistakes When Estimating Your FERS Annuity

Sick leave converted with a shortcut, a high-3 built from the wrong years, the MRA+10 reduction rounded to whole years, a survivor benefit priced off the wrong base, and leftover days dropped too early. Each mistake is small on its own — together they can move an estimate by hundreds of dollars a year.
